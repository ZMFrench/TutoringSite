Math & Physics Tutoring Built on Understanding

Success in math and physics starts with a solid foundation. When students struggle with core concepts, learning advanced topics can be frustrating and overwhelming.

My approach is to uncover and strengthen those foundational gaps first, building confidence and understanding that last well beyond the next quiz or final exam. My goal is not just better grades — it’s lifelong problem-solving skills and self-confidence.
